Any `[~]` that persists must name what would flip it. This is separate from the L0 coverage-cap ruling. It is the lane's own exit criterion, and it is currently never measured.

2. **Make knowledge reachable from decisions, not just from the index.** `corpus/knowledge/` has 81 entries, and all of them are indexed in `corpus/README.md`. But 51 of the 81 are never named anywhere in `SKILL.md` (1,520 lines, about 374 KB). Examples are `benchmark-task-validity-checklist.md`, `contamination-detection-and-transparency.md`, `benchmark-saturation-and-lifecycle.md` and `agent-time-horizon-and-task-validity.md`. An agent that follows SKILL.md's decision tables, which is the lane's stated interface, will never open 63% of the depth the lane has built. Proposal: give every knowledge entry at least one SKILL.md decision row or checklist step that says when to consult it, and add a reachability census (currently 30/81 by filename stem). An entry that is reachable only through the index counts as stored, not as depth. This also gives SHONEN item 1 a measurable form.

3. **Stop stale corpus copies from leaking into retrieval.** `.ufo/` tracks 4,142 files under nested `*/corpus/` paths. For example, there are 35 frozen copies of `corpus/knowledge/ledger-route-telemetry.md`, and 6 `.ufo/**/considerthese/` copies, mostly from round-28 scratch. The repo has no `.ignore` or `.rgignore` (only `.gitignore`), so a plain `rg` or semantic lookup for a knowledge topic returns stale snapshots next to the live entry. That is a quiet form of the bleed SHONEN item 4 forbids. Proposal: (a) add a root `.ignore` that excludes `.ufo/**/corpus/**` and `.ufo/**/scratch/**` from default search; (b) record future BASE/probe snapshots as `commit sha + path` that `git show` can rebuild, not as copied trees; (c) add a per-round census line counting newly tracked `*/corpus/*` paths under `.ufo/`, with a target of 0. Earlier reflections listed the size of `.ufo` (27,666 blobs) as a risk only. This is the concrete harm and the fix.
