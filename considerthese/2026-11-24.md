It never gets the structural fix that its frequency would justify.

The rule would be that any family with 10 or more rows gets a mechanical guard instead of more rows. For citation-anchor, the obvious guard is quote-anchored citations resolved by script (the 11-17 SHONEN idea, generalised to every `file:NN` in knowledge, lessons and registry rows). The 11-21 docket and 11-21 check-command ideas covered a row's next step. This one covers what kind of row it is and whether it has happened before.
