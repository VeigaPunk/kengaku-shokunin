## findings (detail in .ufo/waves/round-72/w1/CounterMutateR72.md)
F1 case-set (and its K9 membership) is a duplicate detector: cases_ok has the same
   sorted(CASE)==sorted(ids) conjunct and feeds the PREDICATE line, so any receipt that
   trips case-set is refused anyway. A K9-less receipt is refused by case-digest-unique
   (8 distinct digests != len(CASES)=9), by case-digest under --work (K9 re-stage absent),
   and by the per-case vector loop. M6 and M6b both accept the genuine receipt and both
   still refuse every K9-less receipt measured here.
F2 blob_ok is implied by the two independent bindings (probe-blob, probe-working-tree):
   if both pass, PROBE_BLOB.sha == blob == PROBE.sha, so blob_ok can never be the
   deciding conjunct. Enumeration above: 9/9 identical rc, identical defect codes.
F3 battery_lines writes 'PROVENANCE env_cleared=True' as a literal; no field of the
   sandbox payload feeds it. The clause is enforceable against a forged receipt but
   cannot ever record a leak; canary_seen (M4) and allowlist_seen (M5) are the real
   environment witnesses.
F4 a duplicated PROVENANCE line is ACCEPTED (first line wins), although the frozen kill
   rule names 'absent/duplicated/outside /venv/' as failures; a second, canary_seen=7
   copy is also accepted (P2, P3).
F5 the receipt's GRADER/INSTRUCTION/MODEL sha lines and the SANDBOX line are never
   compared to lane-derived values; forging all four still exits 0 (P1). The lane files
   themselves are checked against the constants, so the predicate stays true of the
   artifact, but 3 of the 5 digests the receipt prints are unbound (carried from R71).
F6 allowlist_seen counts the 9 names present, not extra keys: an environment that
   inherited the allowlist plus anything else would still print 9/9.
