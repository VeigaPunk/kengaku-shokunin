@0xjunha  Thanks for the thorough audit. I ran a similar scan on the **Terminal-Bench 2.0** leaderboard submissions, which your snapshot doesn't cover, and found the same pattern at a much smaller scale. Sharing it here so the record is in one place.

## Target

- Public TB2 leaderboard submissions in [`harborframework/terminal-bench-2-leaderboard`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/harborframework/terminal-bench-2-leaderboard), limited to those with ATIF `trajectory.json`: **23 submissions, 10,541 trials**.

## Scan boundary

- Tool-call arguments in `trajectory.json`: any URL in the arguments (fetch tools, `curl`/scripts in shell commands) and search-query arguments.
- Sources: `tbench.ai`, `terminal-bench` (repos and mirrors), `laude-institute`, `harbor-framework` / `harborframework`, matched as whole tokens.
- Not covered: tool results, search-result snippets, native logs, and server-side searches that don't record their query. So this is a lower bound, narrower than your boundary.

## On false matches

The benchmark's name also appears in the tool arguments of **308 other TB2 runs** for ordinary reasons:

- a task whose input is the string `"terminal-bench"`;
- `.ics` files with `PRODID:-//Terminal-Bench//…`;
- canary strings copied into files.

Restricting matches to URLs and search queries removed all of those while keeping the 7 above. That might help pre-filter keyword candidates before LLM review.

## Reproducing it

The scan is a deterministic rule (R13) in [tracelint](https://github.com/AshwinUgale/tracelint), an open-source trajectory checker that reads ATIF directly. It runs over the 10,541 trials in about a minute on a laptop. I'm happy to run it on your TB3/TB4 snapshot to cross-check against your 125 relevant cases, or to share the per-trial output.
