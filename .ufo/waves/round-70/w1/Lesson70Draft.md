The live witness is the seven captures plus the three disjoint pid sets; it is evidence the predicate does not demand.

A grader-side change confined to one bundled leg cannot move a frozen vector.
- **K6's reds are exception-reds.** Four of K6's five reds are uncaught `ConnectionError`s that the runner maps to `F` (`plans/round70_c1_nginx_probe.py:485`); the receipt alone cannot distinguish an assertion-red from an exception-red — the `E:` labels survive only in the sandbox captures (`.ufo/waves/round-70/w1/LegWitnessProof.md:204`). K6 leg 4 is not the body assertion and K6 legs 7/8 are not the log checks; K7 and K3 are those witnesses (`LegWitnessProof.md:223-225`). The coverage loop in `check_receipt` is a table invariant, not a receipt guard: with the frozen table, `leg-vector` and `vector-mismatch` force the TEST lines to the frozen vector, so no passing receipt can leave a leg uncovered (CounterMutate70 H4).
