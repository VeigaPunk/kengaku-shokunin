   instrument", not "bwrap ran".
2. **Commit state.** Receipt + sandbox captures are untracked at review time; the claim's
   "committed bytes" status for the receipt itself lands only with the L1 commit, and the
   SKILL.md row move (F4) is still outstanding — the claim sentence is not yet fully discharged

3. **Host-state coupling.** The `LIBRARIES` line is re-derived against the *current*
   `/fast/ComfyUI/.venv` at every check (`plans/round71_c1_hf_probe.py:655-657`); a future venv
   upgrade would retroactively fail the committed receipt with `library-drift` even though the
   battery was genuine. Current versions match (`python=3.13.16 torch=2.14.1+cu130
   transformers=5.21.0 requests=2.34.2`).
