## Profile sensitivity as a controlled configuration effect

All three profiles substitute the same 1435 occurrences; every one of the 1416 changed leaves differs
pairwise between any two profiles (`distinct_origins`/`path_prefix`/`trailing_slash_sensitivity` all
1416/1416). `path_prefix` values append literal `/prefix` after the synthetic host:port (1435 inserted
`/prefix` sequences). `trailing_slash_sensitivity` values end in `/`, so every occurrence followed by
`/` yields a doubled slash: 697 occurrences across 678 leaves contain `:10xxx//` (task 399:
`http://reddit.invalid:10302//user/MarvelsGrantMan136`), and the 738 whole-value occurrences gain a
trailing `/`. `distinct_origins` has no trailing slash: zero `:port//` occurrences. These are
text-substitution consequences of the fixture values; no normalization, no escaping repair, and no
claim that the resulting strings are deployable, valid, or benchmark-faithful at runtime. The 0 count
for WIKIPEDIA (0 raw occurrences) also means that replacement is performed but vacuous on this input.
