## Top 3 lessons / gains from the past 3 days

1. **The request flag is an instrument surface (depth gain, r23 lesson).** `stream:true` bypasses the shim's
   FAIL-marker scan entirely, so the r22 marker bait is structurally inert there; only the pre-split
   `data_inspection_failed` fallthrough survives both modes. The feature set partitions by mode too
   (`body_schema`/`id_in_body` abstain on stream, `stream_span_class` abstains on non-stream), so naively adding
   stream trials to a v2 population silently re-weights a frozen additive rule. The lesson generalizes past this
   shim: any branch in the measured pipeline is its own surface and needs its own declared freeze.
