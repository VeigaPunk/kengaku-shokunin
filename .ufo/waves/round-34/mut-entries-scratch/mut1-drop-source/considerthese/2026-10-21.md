## Self-improvement suggestions (proposals only; nothing acted on)

1. **Push the grader-perturbation findings back into history: re-score past rounds under a hardened extractor.**
   Round 26 measured that the extractor (`grader.py:121-134`) is weak exactly where expected. P8 (D1 distractor-block
   invariance) was forecast at p=0.03. MULTI 14/31 and composite 187/194 come out INDETERMINATE, and the pooled F1/F2
   result of 1802/1819 hides them (`SCORECARD.md` §Round-26 final measured outcomes). The audit stops at "the grader is
   fragile here". Proposal: a frozen counterfactual run. Apply a candidate extractor policy (strict last-fenced-block,
   or reject on more than one JSON block) to the same pinned 1964-response population, and list every *historical* stored verdict
   (r12–r23 batteries) that flips. That turns an instrument audit into a statement about which past conclusions depend on
   parser policy. That's real coverage of the lane's own evidence base, and it lines up with SCORECARD's own planned step
   ("freeze a comparison of extraction policies").

2. **Prior-art preflight as a pre-registration gate, not a mid-round discovery.**
   Novelty has been stuck at 7 for two rounds because prior art turned up *after* building. In r25, cigate 0.2.0 (S041) forced
   the +1 to be withdrawn in the falsify wave. In r26, evalmut (S045) priced the perturbation audit as tooling prior art mid-build.
   Both times the lane spent a full build wave on a construct whose novelty was already gone. Proposal: `plans/round-N-claims.md`
   gets a required `## Prior-art preflight` block with the dated search log (PyPI, GitHub, arXiv, plus the S0xx rows it opens),
   committed *before* the pre-registration freeze. If a hit prices the claim as replication, the claim gets re-aimed before any
   seats are dispatched. A Q-row would make a missing or post-freeze preflight a closure failure. This is distinct from Q9/Q10,
   which police sources *inside* entries.

3. **Seat-yield ledger and wave budget.**
   Waves are huge and lossy. r25 ran 92 build seats plus 87 falsify seats. r26 had three refused 72-seat dispatch attempts, and its
   64-seat falsify wave lost 11 seats to provider-capacity deaths (`.ufo/waves/round-26/falsify-dead-seats.json`). There's no
   measure of which seats changed a landed byte or verdict. The r25 outside reject came from the integrator, not from any of the
   ~180 lane seats. Proposal: append-only `.ufo/waves/yield.tsv` with one row per seat (role family, report path, whether it caused
   a landed correction/supersede/verdict change, with the commit). Report yield per role family each round, and cut or merge
   families that score zero for two rounds. It also gives a principled cap on wave size, so the lane stops paying for
   dispatch refusals and dead seats.

4. **Blind forecasters so calibration can earn credit again.**
   r26 says its P1–P10 are "code-derivable forecasts… no calibration credit is taken" (`plans/round-26-claims.md` §Predictions).
   r25's CritBrierHonesty found the same thing, which means the lane has had no genuine Brier signal for two rounds. Proposal: a
   forecaster seat that sees only the claim text and family definitions, *not* `grader.py` or the fixtures, files the
   probabilities into a hash-pinned file before the build wave, and is scored at close next to the author's code-derived numbers.
   The gap between the two is itself a calibration measurement (how much the code tells you). This is narrower than 10-20 #4,
   which tracks verdict agreement, not forecasts.

1. **Round 25 was rejected even though both claims verified. Truth at landing time is the bar, and r26 made it executable.**
   The outside integrator re-ran C1/C2 over committed bytes and agreed with both. It still ruled REJECTED-HONEST-STOP, for two
   reasons. First, the Gate table at `8ea41f9` cited wave-2 evidence that didn't exist yet (commit 21:44:48 BRT, first wave-2

   first (R26-E1…E11), and Q13/Q14 plus `gate_timing_check.py` check support structurally against commit-tree absence, not
   mutable mtimes. That check reproduces red on r25 and passes on the actual admission commit `d4dfdb0`. Lesson: admission is
   decided by the bytes in the landing commit, and the lane now has a fail-closed check for one of those bytes.

2. **Interval calibration retired the lane's small-n overclaims.**
   r25's `plans/attrib_calibration.py` re-derived all 119 stored score lines from the nine sha256-pinned r21–r23 receipts
   (0 mismatches) and re-issued the verdicts under exact two-sided CP95. r22's `TARGET hyper 10/10 → PASS` became INDETERMINATE
   (lower bound 0.6915, needs n=17), and two `0/0 → FAIL` became UNTESTED. An independent sh+awk oracle and an 18/18 mutant
   selftest back it up. The falsify wave also caught five vacuous specificity PASSes (PASS 12 → 7). Lesson: "all correct" at
   n≈10 isn't a pass at an 0.80 bar. And even the re-verdict depends on the population (it's PASS under the both-blocks
   reading), so the dedup key belongs in the pre-registration.

3. **The lane audited its own grader, and the scores held honestly.**

   verdicts across 9 MP/MC families, and 909 policy captures, with an independent oracle (`plans/fixtures/r26/perturb_oracle.py`),
