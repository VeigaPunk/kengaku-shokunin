| mode | (a) found | (a2) found | (b) found |

The cause is column-wrap hyphenation, and it is **two-sided**: the same page carries soft hyphens to be dropped ("publicly re- leased", "hidden card imple- mentations", "preserves eval- uation") *and* genuine compounds whose hyphen happens to land at the wrap ("a text- based trading card game", "a contamination- resistant design"). A join-without-hyphen policy mangles the second class; a keep-hyphen policy mangles the first. Neither policy alone admits all three quotes (verified).

Canonical form used here, and the form a byte-substring check must declare for PDF artifacts:

1. delete `-\s*\n\s*` (a hyphen at a wrap point: join the two halves **without** a hyphen),
2. collapse all whitespace runs to one space,
3. delete every remaining `-` (hyphen-insensitive comparison).

Under this form all three quotes occur **exactly once**, and only on the page reported. Reproducible script + receipt: `/tmp/r30-R30SrcS083/verify.py`, output `/tmp/r30-R30SrcS083/verify.out`, `exit=0`:
