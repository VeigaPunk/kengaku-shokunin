## Round83 criterion — quote annotations are derived from retained bytes, including corrections

Proposed Q60 (next unused Q-series id in the QUALITY.md inspected this round; L1 owns final numbering). This extends Q3/Q10/Q50 rather than replacing their claim-scope, second-seat and append-only obligations.

- **Q60 — each newly written or correcting source row and its round Gate derive quoted-word annotations and line anchors mechanically from retained source bytes, with explicit normalization; the annotations of a correction receive the same check as the original.** A whitespace-token count is `len(quote.split())`, not visual estimation; a line range must actually contain the quoted substring. Shell continuations, Markdown display normalization and partial-line quotations are declared, never silently removed. A new row has its URL, access date and a located quote of at most 25 words in the Gate, including historical rows whose Gate omission is being corrected. A failed fetch has an HTTP response anchor, never an invented source-content anchor. Old rows stay byte-identical; a correction is a superseding row (Q34).

Grounding: round-81 S260/S261's 9/14/17 annotations required S265/S266's 7/12/13 corrections; round-82 S262/S263 nevertheless introduced 6 and 1 where the exact fresh-fetched quote lengths are 4 and 2. Round-82 Gate also omitted the corrective S265/S266 rows. These are repeated annotation mistakes, not a new grader defect. Q3 gives the quote length/scope obligation; Q10 requires another reader; Q50 requires re-derivation before citation. None of those texts specifically requires the correction's own quote/count/anchor and its per-row Gate coverage to be priced together. The proposed addition is that bounded mechanical obligation, not another research method.
