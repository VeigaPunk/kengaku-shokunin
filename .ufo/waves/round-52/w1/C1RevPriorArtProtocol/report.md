# C1RevPriorArtProtocol — R52-C1 review

## Standard and sources

- **Quantity:** Frozen C1 leg 4 requires at least **3 distinct URLs** in `corpus/evidence/round52/prior-art.md` (claims `plans/round-52-claims.md:65-68`). The scout's dispatch separately requires **at least 5 public sources**; use that stricter build requirement. The lane precedent does not define one universal quota: r9 used about 20 searches and fetched every cited primary (`corpus/knowledge/eval-prior-art-calibration.md:3-9`); r21 reports 9 fetched primaries plus 2 searches (`corpus/knowledge/attrib-prior-art-r21.md:158-164`). Every cited primary must be fetched, not merely named.
- **Fetched URL/source:** a public primary actually opened during this round, with its HTTP status recorded, whose relevant content was read in the returned bytes. Search snippets/synthesis are leads, not evidence (`eval-prior-art-calibration.md:3-9`; `attrib-prior-art-r21.md:182-206`, especially line 205). Record URL, status, access date, and the exact source anchor. A negative must enumerate its bounded search surface and outcomes; it is a search-set result, not proof no publication exists (`attrib-prior-art-r21.md:158-181`).
- **Located quote:** verbatim text of **≤25 words** with a precise location (section/page/file:line/comment line and revision), taken from the fetched bytes. SOURCES contract v1 rule 1 (`/home/vgpnk/Projects/shokunin/.ufo/waves/kaika/SOURCES-CONTRACT-v1.md:45-48`) and QUALITY Q3 (`corpus/QUALITY.md:31-35`) require an anchored quote and say a paraphrase may narrow, never widen, it. The C1 scout brief requires this for each source and requires that it address whole-benchmark triage and V1 presence-only/V4 sparse-test weaknesses.
- **Verdict discipline:** adversarial default is `known` until a source fails to cover the finding (`eval-prior-art-calibration.md:8-9`; `attrib-prior-art-r21.md:11-13`). `phenomenon-known` needs a fetched primary and located quote that documents the weakness, with its scope stated. `phenomenon-new` needs a bounded, logged search that found no fetched primary covering the weakness; phrase it as “not found in this fetched/search set,” not an exhaustive negative. Keep **phenomenon** and **instrument** pricing separate: r9 says defensible novelty can be instrument-level without being phenomenon-level (`eval-prior-art-calibration.md:30-41`); r21 explicitly splits them (`attrib-prior-art-r21.md:27-75, 90-136`). Label self-reports and adjacent/partial evidence honestly (QUALITY Q5, `corpus/QUALITY.md:42-47`).

## Application to R52-C1 and L1 acceptance checklist

The claim is a population-level instrument (triage of the frozen 89-task/184-grader population) and findings are task-level grader weaknesses. Prior-art must price both levels, but the frozen score rule is specifically per confirmed finding: novelty 8→9 only if at least one is `phenomenon-new` (`plans/round-52-claims.md:94-97`). An instrument-new conclusion alone does **not** satisfy that rule. Known weak-grader and benchmark-audit work is a serious neighbor to inspect, not to wave away: the lane already records OpenAI's self-reported test-validity audits (S005/S006, `corpus/SOURCES.md:5-6`; summarized in `corpus/knowledge/agent-time-horizon-and-task-validity.md:17-19`), and R44's prior-art review discusses Terminal Wrench, RDI and other audit neighbors (`.ufo/waves/round-44/wave/R44ScoutPriorArt.md`, Findings F1/F6 and Limits). A source stating the weakness class can establish phenomenon-known even if it does not name this task; the file must distinguish class-level coverage from task-instance coverage rather than silently calling an undocumented instance a new phenomenon.

**Mechanical-counter caveat (actionable):** `prior_art()` only computes `len(set(URL.findall(file_text))) >= 3` (`plans/round52_c1_grader_audit.py:42, 213-218`); it does not verify fetches, status, quotes, or per-finding verdict coverage. Its URL regex retains terminal `>` and `.`, so one URL written as `https://example.org/a`, `https://example.org/a.`, and `<https://example.org/a>` produces **3 distinct matches**. I ran a small Python reproduction of the exact regex and observed those three strings; therefore `PRIOR-ART ok=True urls=3` is not proof of three fetched primaries. Count canonical primary documents manually. The r21 note itself has only 3 regex matches despite reporting 9 fetched primaries, because its receipt uses a loop URL and its table gives arXiv IDs (`attrib-prior-art-r21.md:76-88, 182-206`); R52 should include literal source URLs.
